# Impact of AI on Career & Employment

## 📌 Project Overview

This portfolio project analyzes survey responses to understand how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is influencing career opportunities, employment perceptions, productivity, skill requirements, AI adoption, and preparedness for the future of work.

The analysis is designed as a practical **Data Analyst / Business Intelligence case study**, moving from survey data collection and preparation to analysis, KPI development, visualization, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

AI adoption is changing how people work and study. Organizations, educational institutions, students, and professionals need to understand:

- How frequently people use AI tools
- Which AI applications are considered most useful
- Whether people believe AI will create or displace jobs
- How concerned respondents are about AI replacing their current role or study area
- Which soft skills are expected to become more valuable
- Whether respondents have formal AI/ML training
- How prepared employers and institutions are for AI
- What barriers are limiting AI adoption
- Whether respondents expect AI to influence their career or study field

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Measure AI adoption and usage frequency.
2. Analyze familiarity with current AI tools.
3. Identify the most useful AI applications.
4. Understand perceptions of AI-driven job creation.
5. Analyze concern about AI replacing current roles or study areas.
6. Identify the soft skills perceived as most valuable in an AI-driven job market.
7. Evaluate AI/ML training and certification patterns.
8. Analyze perceived AI preparedness of employers and institutions.
9. Identify major barriers to AI adoption.
10. Examine the expected likelihood of career/study-field change due to AI.
11. Translate survey responses into actionable business and education insights.

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The analytical workflow includes:

1. Import survey responses.
2. Inspect column names and data types.
3. Remove email identifiers from the public analytical dataset.
4. Standardize categorical response values.
5. Convert rating-style fields into analysis-friendly numeric values where appropriate.
6. Create calculated measures in Power BI using DAX.
7. Validate response counts and category distributions.
8. Build visuals around business questions rather than isolated survey questions.

## 💡 Selected Dataset Observations

In the 100-response synthetic dataset:

- Students represent **40%** of responses.
- Working professionals represent **36%**.
- Daily AI usage is reported by **36 respondents**.
- Weekly AI usage is reported by **27 respondents**.
- Monthly AI usage is reported by **17 respondents**.
- **11 respondents** report never using AI.
- **9 respondents** report rarely using AI.

These figures describe this **synthetic portfolio dataset only** and should not be presented as representative of the wider student or professional population.
